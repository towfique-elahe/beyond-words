\chapter*{AI ASSISTANCE DISCLOSURE}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{AI ASSISTANCE DISCLOSURE}

We used AI tools during the preparation of this thesis for the following
purposes:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em]
  \item \textbf{Research/Idea Generation:} AI tools were used to discuss the
    research direction, to assess the feasibility of the lexical (word-level)
    route before it was built, to clarify concepts related to self-supervised
    speech encoders and speech recognition, and to locate and check
    bibliographic records. The research questions, the choice of models, the
    evaluation protocol and all methodological decisions were made by ourselves.
  \item \textbf{Coding/Technical Work:} AI tools assisted with Python
    programming throughout the experimental work, including the data pipeline,
    the fine-tuning and evaluation scripts, the Kaggle notebooks, the
    transcription and lexicon scripts, the statistical analysis and the figure
    scripts. All AI-assisted code was reviewed, run and verified by ourselves,
    and every number reported in this thesis was produced by that code on our
    data.
  \item \textbf{Writing/Editing:} AI tools were used to draft and revise chapter
    text from our experimental results and notes, to prepare the \LaTeX{}
    manuscript, and for grammar and clarity. All AI-assisted writing was
    reviewed, critically revised and finalised by ourselves. AI tools were not
    used to generate experimental results or data.
  \item \textbf{Other Assistance:} AI tools assisted in building the public
    demonstration application and the defence presentation.
\end{itemize}

\noindent AI Tool(s) Used: Claude Code (Anthropic).

\medskip
\noindent We confirm that all AI-generated or AI-assisted outputs used in this
thesis were reviewed and critically evaluated by us. AI-assisted code was tested
and verified before use. We remain responsible for the dataset preparation, the
training and evaluation of every model, the analysis, and the written content of
this thesis.
