Which Bangla dialect is this?

Speak for five seconds or more, or type a sentence. The sound route listens to how you speak; the words route reads what you say. Nine classes: Barishal, Chattogram, Dhaka, Khulna, Mymensingh, Noakhali, Rajshahi, Sylhet and the Standard register.

What this can and cannot do

  • The sound model is a Whisper-medium encoder fine-tuned on 13 hours of Bangladeshi television, film and online speech. The thesis model scores 0.984 macro-F1 on a conventional test split and 0.919 on a split where no training speaker appears.
  • This page serves a microphone-robust variant trained with channel augmentation: 0.896 on that strict split, but 0.84 instead of 0.39 on clips passed through a simulated laptop microphone. Recordings are also level-normalised before scoring. A noisy room, a far microphone or read speech in the standard register will still push it towards Standard.
  • The words route uses regional word lists and a text classifier trained on transcripts of the same corpus; the transcript comes from the Tugstugi (Ben-10) recogniser, which keeps about one dialect word in four.
  • Speakers from regions outside the nine classes are mapped to the nearest class the model knows.
